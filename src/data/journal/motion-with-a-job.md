The easiest way to add motion to a portfolio is to add more motion.

The harder—and usually better—problem is deciding what should stay still.

A project showcase needs enough movement to feel alive, but a case study needs enough quiet space to be understood. A blog needs an even calmer rhythm. The interface should change its intensity depending on what the visitor is trying to do.

## Three levels

**Signature motion** is memorable. The sketchbook page turn belongs here.

**Navigation motion** communicates state. Menus opening, active links changing, filters responding, and transitions between sections can use small, fast cues.

**Content motion** should be almost invisible. A paragraph should not fight the reader for attention.

## Accessibility is part of the motion system

Reduced-motion preferences should not be treated as an afterthought. When motion is optional, the information hierarchy should remain intact without it.

That means the page turn can become a simple page switch, tilt can disappear, and decorative transitions can shorten or stop while the same content remains usable.
