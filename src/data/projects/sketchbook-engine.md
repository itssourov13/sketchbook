## The brief

Turn a visually rich sketchbook landing page into a durable piece of a larger personal website without losing the original interaction language.

The baseline combines curved page turns, a draggable magnifier, pointer tilt, zoom controls, keyboard navigation, responsive layout, and a tactile paper aesthetic.

## What changed

The experience was first verified against its source and assets, then moved into an Astro foundation. The interaction is now isolated as a reusable Sketchbook component instead of being the entire application shell.

That separation is the important part: the signature experience can evolve independently while the rest of the site gains normal pages for work, writing, and about content.

## Why it matters

A signature interaction is most useful when it has a clear job. Here, the sketchbook is the invitation into the site; the content system carries the deeper story.
