## The brief

The sketchbook began as a visual experiment and became a permanent room inside the site. Its job is not to explain the writing, but to give the eye another way to slow down before the next page.

Curved page turns, a draggable magnifier, pointer tilt, zoom controls, keyboard navigation, responsive layout, and a tactile paper surface keep the interaction physical without making it loud.

## What changed

The original interaction and its visual language were preserved, then given a quieter home inside the larger site. The page turn, magnifier, tilt, and zoom can now evolve on their own while the writing remains free to stay simple.

That separation matters because the sketchbook works best as a pause: something tactile between pages of text, not a performance the rest of the site has to imitate.

## Why it matters

Words are not the only way to pay attention. Sometimes a page, a texture, or the slow turn of an image can do the same work. The sketchbook keeps that quieter language open.
